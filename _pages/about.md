I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at the University of Alberta, where I also completed my Ph.D. My research develops **AI-driven solutions for intelligent, resilient, and sustainable energy systems**, with applications in buildings, greenhouses, data centers, renewable energy integration, and grid-interactive infrastructure.

Methodologically, my work spans the **Internet of Things**, **predictive modeling**, **optimization**, and **uncertainty quantification**. A central thread of my Ph.D. research was probabilistic building power demand prediction under **data scarcity** — reducing the data needed to train reliable models by roughly 95% compared to conventional machine learning approaches.

I am currently on the academic job market, seeking a tenure-track faculty position combining research and teaching.
